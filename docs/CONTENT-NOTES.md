## How the facts are used

- **Fees and durations** are shown as "published fee" or "reference" with the check date and a link to the source. They are never presented as live prices, and no seat count or start date is shown for any Sales Ladder level.
- **Programme descriptions** are paraphrased summaries of who each level is for and its stated focus. No module lists, curricula or assessments were invented.
- **The seven stages** keep ABC's published names and order. The one-line descriptions on the Sales Ladder page were written for this concept, and the page says so.
- **Launch Lab:** the published 1 October 2026 start has passed, so the page says so and shows no future intake. The interest form asks only for the enquirer's details and warns against sharing a young person's date of birth or school.
- **November cohort and masterclass:** not shown. Neither could be tied to a specific programme or confirmed as still open, and both would date the concept quickly.
- **Careers:** the official listing has no date, so it is not reproduced. Four sample roles name no employer and no salary, and each carries "Demo opportunity — not an active vacancy."
- **Recruitment support** is presented as a service because the careers page shows ABC placing sales staff with other organisations. The homepage does not list it as a separate service.
